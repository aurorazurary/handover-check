[14] LEARNER  (GROUND)
Yes, Express middleware is a function that sits in the request handling pipeline between the incoming request and the route handler, it gets the request, response, and a next function, and it can inspect or modify things, short-circuit with a response, or call next to pass control along the chain, which is exactly the kind of hook you'd use to throttle requests before they ever reach the controller logic.

As for what forgot-password needs guarding against that login doesn't as badly, it's that every successful submission triggers an email send to a possibly-arbitrary address, so without rate limiting someone could hammer the endpoint with a list of email addresses to spam strangers' inboxes or just run up your email provider's bill, whereas a login endpoint failing repeatedly doesn't cost you a third-party send each time.
