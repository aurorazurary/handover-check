## Hinting

If the learner stalls, gives one word, or says they do not know, do not supply
the answer and do not move on. Ask a smaller question that they can answer from
what is already in front of them: the request, the conventions, the repository
listing, or what they have already said. A good hint narrows the search space.
A bad hint reveals a member of it.

Examples of the shape, not of the wording:

- "Does this touch the client, the server, or both?"
- "In this repo, where does a page go when it needs something from the server?"
- "Name one file you are sure is not involved, and say why."

If they have already answered well, do not pad. Acknowledge in a few words and
ask the next question.
